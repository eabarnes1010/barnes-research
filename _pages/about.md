We are the **Barnes Research Group**, led by [Prof. Elizabeth Barnes](/prof-barnes/) and based in [Computing &amp; Data Sciences](https://www.bu.edu/cds-faculty/) and [Earth &amp; Environment](https://www.bu.edu/earth/) at [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/). We study Earth system variability, predictability, and change across time and space, developing **interpretable AI** tools for scientific discovery.

**We're hiring postdocs!** We have two open postdoctoral positions in Earth system data science:

- **AI & Statistics for Earth Simulations**: combining AI with causality, information theory, and statistics to improve simulation of the coupled Earth system and quantify its uncertainty.
- **AI for Near-Term Climate and Society**: coupling AI Earth system simulations with societal, health, and economic models to study near-term climate trajectories, atmospheric perturbations, and proposed climate interventions.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. See the [full ad](/assets/pdf/postdoc-positions-2026.pdf) for qualifications and how to apply.
