We are the **Barnes Research Group**, led by [Prof. Elizabeth (Libby) Barnes](/prof-barnes/) and based in [Computing &amp; Data Sciences](https://www.bu.edu/cds-faculty/) and [Earth &amp; Environment](https://www.bu.edu/earth/) at [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/). We study Earth system variability, predictability, and change across time and space, developing **interpretable AI** tools for scientific discovery.

Take a look at our [research](/research/), meet the [group](/group/), watch our [whiteboard animations](/scicomm/), and browse our [publications](/publications/).
